## New discovery and what it does not establish

Issue83 matches the mean pairwise distance in seven standardized physical coordinates,
not their distribution among coordinates. An exploratory audit of2400 selections found
increased x/y translation variance and decreased blur/noise/brightness variance.

Issue86 tests an alternative score: choose subsets using translation variance alone,
under the same hardness and scalar-total-diversity constraints. Its positive high-low
contrast shows that an informative physical-factor score can produce a performance
contrast without using gradient novelty as the selection score.

However, translation-based selection also increases reference gradient novelty:
+0.105610 versus+0.208500 for the gradient-based selection family. Gradient-based
selection also increases translation variance. The variables remain coupled.
The result therefore does NOT demonstrate a causal path independent of gradient geometry.
It also does not establish that translation is the only useful factor.

## Other evidence and negative results retained

The original CIFAR primary40-pair online comparison supports mean+0.1206 pp with
Holm(5)p0.01336; it does not confirm p10/minimum safety. Tested MLP/CNN comparisons,
optimizer replication, RNG compression and coordinate-relevance studies remain in
their original documents. These do not establish universally good seed integers.

Gradient novelty did not beat random selection as a mean-gradient estimator, and
loss-hard can make a larger immediate one-step loss decrease. Pure diversity and
larger accumulated gradient rank are insufficient explanations.

Raw representation effective rank changes under function-preserving reparameterization,
so it is not an intrinsic functional mediator. Its fixed-parameterization predictive
record remains a marker rather than a calibrated per-run gate. Standardized-rank
budget mediation failed (one-sided p0.5063).

The structured/nuisance matching program repeatedly failed feasibility, and the
near-clean matched test returned NO SHARED REPLICATION. Reusable-factor causality
remains unproven. MLP full-minus-clean specificity replicated on reserve images but
was not reproduced by SmallCNN; no universal shift-strength or monotone budget law.
The new interventions' clean/tail results remain secondary, not a general safety result.

## Verification and uncertainty

Issue83:60 states,30 schedules,2400 reference rows and4800 environment rows verified;
max independent reaggregation difference1.11e-16.
Issue86:150 states,30 schedules,4800 reference rows and12000 environment rows verified;
source bytes and ZIP digests checked; frozen aggregate recomputation difference0.0;
translation-score reconstruction difference at most4.44e-16.
Independent public paired checkers reproduce both frozen decisions without retraining.

The statistical units are paired training repetitions, not individual environments.
Image identities and environment pools are fixed within each study. Paired SE is
not a total uncertainty including dataset/model/backend variation. Both new studies
use deterministic one-thread CPU execution; the historical CIFAR cross-run audit
still shows that cross-hardware bitwise reproducibility is not established.
No GPU or controlled-clock efficiency claim is made.

## Current claim and next discrimination

Useful subset selection is supported in the tested regimes. Shared-reference fixed-budget
schedule interventions remain positive after balancing two scalar summaries. A
translation-only scoring alternative is also positive, but its gradient geometry
changes simultaneously. The strongest mechanistic interpretation is now a candidate
factor-allocation explanation, not unique gradient-novelty causality.

Next: test whether gradient novelty can be varied while balancing spatial allocation
as well as reference hardness and scalar total diversity. A separate training-only
feasibility stage is necessary before a fresh confirmatory block. Downstream functional
mediation, cross-dataset transfer of these specific interventions, tail safety and
matched-cost practical deployment remain open.
